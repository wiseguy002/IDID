iDID fact sheet

idid.care · press@idid.care

What it is

A free, private journal for people living with dissociation, DID and trauma responses, and the families and friends who support them.

The problem

Dissociation can cause lost time and memory gaps. People struggle to recall and explain what happened, what triggered it and what helped, which makes care and support harder.

What iDID does

  • Quick, optional logging: when, where, triggers, mood, parts present, lost time, duration, who noticed
  • Timeline with search and filters, and "looking back" reflections added later
  • Trust circle observations from family and friends, with invite status and reminders
  • Joining a circle after starting alone merges the person's journal into the profile made for them, with no duplicates
  • Per-person access chosen by the circle's owner (view and add, view only, or selected information), and handing the circle to the person it's about
  • Therapist and care-team sharing: scoped, read-only, explicitly consented, always current, and removable at any time
  • Patterns and clinician-ready summaries
  • Pause tools (breathing, box breathing, grounding), a wordless Calm space, gentle check-ins and a daily act of kindness
  • Mood Garden: one plant per part (or per person, including family members) that grows only from care, with weather that follows check-ins, seasons, and gentle ideas that link to the rest of iDID
  • Who's here: a two-tap fronting log with front history, switch patterns, and inner-board notes surfaced when the part they're for arrives
  • What did I miss?: grounding plus everything noted in iDID during a period of lost time
  • Voice notes (up to two minutes, end-to-end encrypted) and an optional weekly reflection
  • Supporting someone: a guide for partners, family and friends with words to try
  • Find support by ZIP code, and saved support contacts pinned to the Pause screen
  • Appointment reminders with calendar export; therapy prep with a "since last session" summary
  • Safety plan, "Check on me", comfort kit, inner board for parts, body basics with medication reminders, therapy prep, quick hide
  • Anonymous, curated kind notes between people using iDID
  • A quick tour, Help & guides, Explore with gentle milestone badges (no streaks), and What's new after each update
  • Restore on a new device with an email code and passphrase; works offline and syncs when back online
  • Printable clinician summary, spreadsheet export and full backup
  • "Suggest a feature" with replies from the iDID team, "Help shape iDID" for volunteers who want to try new features early, and "Tell a friend"
  • English and Español (beta): setup, sign-in, Home, Pause, the safety plan, What did I miss? and Spanish crisis options (988, press 2; text AYUDA to 988)

Privacy

  • Encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM) with the person's passphrase plus a recovery code
  • End-to-end encrypted sharing: servers store only ciphertext
  • When a circle's owner removes someone, the circle's encryption key is changed, so the removed person's old copy can't open anything new
  • Sign-in protected by a bot check and rate limits
  • No ads, no trackers, no data sales; notifications never reveal details. Optional anonymous counts of which screens are used are off unless a person turns them on, and never include anything they write

Accessibility & sensory design

Calm palette with dark and high-contrast themes, four text sizes, calm-motion mode, extra-readable font option, large tap targets, and no sounds, vibration or flashing.

Availability

Free web app at app.idid.care for phones, tablets and computers (installable to the home screen), in English and Spanish (beta). Optional contributions through Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/ididcare) never unlock features. Information for clinicians at idid.care/clinicians. Android and iOS apps planned. Launched November 2026.

Important

iDID is a journaling and support tool, not a medical device. In a crisis in the US, call or text 988; in an emergency, call 911.
