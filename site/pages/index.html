Why iDID

When time goes missing, it helps to have a place to put the pieces.

Dissociation can mean lost time, feeling far away, or not knowing who was "here" for part of the day. Trauma responses can arrive without warning. Afterwards it's hard to remember what happened, let alone explain it to a therapist or a worried partner.

iDID gives you and your circle a calm, private way to write down what was noticed (when, where, what might have set it off, how it felt, and which parts were present) so patterns become easier to see, and support becomes easier to find.

Features

Gentle tools that fit real, hard days

Note a moment in a few taps

When and where, possible triggers, mood, who was fronting or co-fronting, lost time, how long, and who noticed. Every step is optional.

Invite family and friends, see who has joined, and remind anyone who missed it. Choose what each person can see and do, and hand the circle to the person it's about whenever they're ready.

Patterns that make sense

Breathing, box breathing that builds a little group of boxes, grounding, and a "right now" reminder of where you are. A wordless Calm space of ripples and drifting lights. Kind check-ins suggest a breath, your own ritual, or some fresh air.

Mood Garden

A plant for each part, or one for you, grown by the care you give: check-ins, pauses, kind notes, moments noticed. Choose plants and names. Weather follows how you've been, so hard days bring wind and rain, but nothing ever wilts. Gentle ideas help it grow, and it shows how far you've come.

Private by design

Your story stays yours.

What you write in iDID is encrypted on your own device with a passphrase only you know. When you share with your circle, it's encrypted end-to-end before it leaves your phone, so not even we can read it.

How we protect you
  • Encrypted on your device with AES-256 and your passphrase, plus a recovery code only you hold.
  • End-to-end encrypted sharing. Our servers only ever see scrambled data.
  • "Only me" entries stay private, even from your circle.
  • Notifications never reveal details, just a gentle nudge to open the app.
  • Quick hide: one tap locks iDID and shows an ordinary-looking screen.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling data. Ever.

How it works

Start in a minute. Go at your own pace.

No account is needed to begin. Your journal works on your device straight away. Connect your circle whenever you're ready.

  1. Open iDID and choose a passphrase

    On your phone, add it to your home screen. Choose comfortable colours, text size and calm motion.

  2. Note moments when they happen

    Or later. A best guess is fine. One word is enough.

  3. Invite your trust circle

    Send a one-time private link, choose what each person can see, and see when they've joined.

  4. Notice patterns and share with your care team

    Give your therapist a private, read-only view, or bring a clear summary to your next appointment.

Questions

Questions people ask

What is iDID?

iDID (said "I did") is a free, private journal for people living with dissociation, dissociative identity disorder (DID), OSDD and trauma responses, and for the family, friends and therapists who support them. It helps you note moments that felt different, see patterns over time, and share what helps with people you trust.

Is iDID free?

Yes. iDID is free to use, with no ads, no trackers and no selling of data. It runs in any modern web browser and can be added to your phone's home screen like an app.

Who can see my journal?

Only you, unless you choose to share. Everything is encrypted on your device with your passphrase. If you connect a trust circle, entries are end-to-end encrypted, so our servers only ever store scrambled data. Moments you mark "only me" stay private even from your circle.

Can my therapist see my journal?

Only if you choose to share with them. You pick exactly what they can see, such as patterns, moments, your safety plan or therapy prep notes, and agree to a plain summary first. They open a private, read-only link with no account needed, and you can remove their access at any time.

Is iDID only for people with DID?

No. iDID is for anyone who experiences dissociation, such as losing time, feeling unreal or detached, or switching between parts, and for trauma responses like freezing or flashbacks. You can use your own words: parts, alters, headmates or selves, or none at all.

Can families and partners use iDID?

Yes. Family members, partners and friends can join a trust circle to add what they noticed, which can fill in gaps in memory. The circle's owner chooses what each person can see and do, and can hand the circle to the person it's about whenever they're ready.

What if I forget my passphrase?

Use the recovery code you saved when you set iDID up. Nobody, including us, can reset your passphrase, which is what keeps your journal private.

Is iDID a medical device?

No. iDID is a journaling and support tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition and isn't a replacement for professional care. In an emergency, call 911. For crisis support in the US, call or text 988.
