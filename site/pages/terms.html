Not medical care, and not for emergencies

iDID helps you notice, record and share what's happening. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it isn't a substitute for a doctor, therapist or other professional. Nothing in iDID, including its guides, care ideas and Learn pages, is medical advice.

iDID doesn't watch what you write and can't send help. Reminders and "Check on me" depend on your device, your connection and other people, so they can fail. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. For crisis support in the US, call or text 988.

Who can use iDID

You must be at least 13 years old. If you're under 18, we encourage you to use iDID with the support of a trusted adult, such as a parent, guardian or counselor, if that feels safe for you. You can use iDID on your own device without an account. The online circle needs an email address you can receive codes at.

You're responsible for what you share with your circle or through a shared view, and for keeping your passphrase and recovery code safe. If both are lost, nobody can recover your journal, including us.
