iDID

A gentle, private journal for dissociation and trauma responses, for individuals, families and trust circles.

iDID is a journaling and support tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call 911. For crisis support in the US, call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741.
