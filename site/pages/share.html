Messages you can send

To a friend

To family or a partner

I've started using iDID to keep track of hard moments. If you notice something, it can ask you to add what you saw, which really helps me fill in the gaps. Would you join my circle? I'll send you a private invite link. https://idid.care

To a therapist or clinician

I'm using iDID (https://idid.care) to log dissociative moments, triggers and what helps between sessions. If it would help, I can share a private, read-only view of what I choose with you. It stays up to date between sessions, and no account is needed to open it.

For a support group or community

Sharing a free tool some of us might find helpful: iDID (https://idid.care) is a calm, private journal for people living with dissociation, DID and trauma responses, and the people who support them. It's encrypted, ad-free, and built with sensory needs in mind.
